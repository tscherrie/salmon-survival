Direct parts and manual download

Source manifest · Complete ZIP checksums · Part checksums · Download and verification script

On macOS or Linux, download the script, review it, then run:

sh download-sources.sh

The script downloads both archives without credentials, checks all parts, joins them in the stated order and checks each final ZIP. On another system, save the listed parts, concatenate their bytes in numeric order, and compare the resulting ZIP's SHA-256 with the manifest. Joining parts is byte concatenation; extracting each part separately will not work.
